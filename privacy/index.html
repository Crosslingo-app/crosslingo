CrossLingo Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 5, 2026 · Version 1.3

1. Summary

CrossLingo ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is a translation app for iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use text translation, voice translation, camera translation, image translation, feedback, attribution, analytics, and CrossLingo Plus subscription features.

CrossLingo does not require you to create an account. The App uses Apple system frameworks for speech recognition, translation, camera capture, photo selection, text recognition, and in-app purchases.

We do not operate a server that receives or processes your typed translation text, microphone audio, speech transcripts, translation results, photos, or conversation history for translation processing. Translation features primarily rely on Apple system frameworks and locally available language resources on your device.

2. Translation Content We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, sell, or share the following information through our own translation services:

  • Voice recordings or live microphone audio
  • Speech transcripts, translations, or conversation history
  • Photos or images captured through the camera for translation
  • Text you type into the App for translation

3. Subscription and App Functionality Information

To provide CrossLingo Plus, restore purchases, verify subscriptions, and diagnose purchase issues, the App may send information such as:

  • App-generated identifiers, app instance ID, IDFV
  • Device model, operating system version, App version, language, region, and time zone
  • Subscription product information, Apple transaction identifiers, subscription status, and expiration time
  • Technical request information used for service operation and troubleshooting

We do not sell this information.

4. Feedback and Customer Support

If you submit feedback through the App, we may collect:

  • Feedback content
  • Optional contact information
  • Optional images attached by you
  • App and device information required for troubleshooting

Please do not include sensitive personal information, private conversations, payment card details, passwords, or confidential content in feedback.

5. Apple System Services

The App uses Apple-provided frameworks to provide translation, speech recognition, camera, image selection, text recognition, and subscription functionality.

Depending on your device, iOS version, language, and system settings, Apple services may process requests on device or through Apple-operated services.

We do not store or use content processed by Apple frameworks for purposes unrelated to providing translation features and app functionality.

6. Attribution, Analytics, and Reporting

The App may use AppsFlyer, Meta/Facebook SDK, and our reporting endpoint for install attribution, marketing measurement, app analytics, and subscription funnel diagnostics.

These services may process identifiers and app interaction events, but must not receive translation content, including text translation input, voice recordings, transcripts, translation results, photos used for translation, or conversation history.

7. Local Storage

The App may store local settings and app state on your device, including selected languages, onboarding state, subscription information cache, usage limits, local history, and feedback read status.

8. Purchases and Subscriptions

CrossLingo Plus is provided through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes payment information and manages billing, renewal, cancellation, and refunds.

We do not receive your full payment card details.

9. Data Retention

Local app data can be removed by deleting the App from your device. Subscription records and feedback records may be retained as needed for subscription management, customer support, fraud prevention, and legal obligations.

10. Third-Party Services

CrossLingo uses third-party services to support app functionality, subscription management, analytics, attribution measurement, and service improvement.

  • Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • AppsFlyer Privacy Policy: https://www.appsflyer.com/legal/privacy-policy/
  • Meta Privacy Policy: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policies/

11. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The Last Updated date will be revised when material changes are made.
