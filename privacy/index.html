CrossTranslate: AI Translator Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 20, 2026 · Version 1.7

Introduction

Welcome to CrossTranslate: AI Translator ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use translation features, Live Translator Pro, analytics services, and Feedback.

No Account Required

CrossTranslate: AI Translator does not require an account. We may use app-generated identifiers and service-related identifiers for subscription verification, purchase restoration, Feedback, analytics, security, and technical support.

Translation Content

The App uses Apple system frameworks and language resources on your device to provide translation functions. We do not collect, sell, or share translation text, voice recordings, speech transcripts, translation results, or images used for translation through our own translation processing services.

The App does not provide translation history or favorites features and does not intentionally retain translation results as persistent records.

Subscription Information

To provide Live Translator Pro, verify purchases, restore subscriptions, and diagnose issues, we may process subscription information, transaction identifiers, app identifiers, device information, app version, and technical request information.

Apple processes payment information. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Feedback Feature

Users may submit Feedback through Settings. Feedback History allows users to view submitted feedback and developer replies.

We may process feedback content, optional contact information, optional images, device information, app version, developer replies, and unread status used for Feedback notification indicators.

Feedback is used to investigate issues, reply to users, improve the App, maintain support records, and display reply notifications.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party providers for subscriptions, analytics, attribution, Feedback services, hosting, security, and technical operations.

We require service providers processing information on our behalf to provide the same or equivalent level of protection described in this policy and required by applicable laws.

Analytics

The App may use AppsFlyer, Meta/Facebook SDK, and internal reporting services to measure installs, app events, and subscription performance.

These services are not intended to receive translation content.

Local Storage

The App may store local settings, downloaded language resources, subscription status cache, and Feedback notification status on your device.

Data Retention

Subscription and Feedback records may be retained as reasonably necessary for service operation, support, security, fraud prevention, and legal obligations.

The App does not currently provide an in-app deletion function for individual Feedback records.
