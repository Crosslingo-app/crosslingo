CrossLingo Support

Last Updated: August 6, 2026 · Version 1.4

CrossLingo is a translation app for text, voice, camera, and image translation on iPhone. This page provides support information for CrossLingo Version 1.4, Live Translator Pro, and the in-app Feedback feature.

Common Help Topics

Voice Translation

Make sure Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions are enabled for CrossLingo in the iOS Settings app.

Siri and Dictation Message

If iOS reports that Siri and Dictation are disabled, enable Siri and Dictation in the system settings and check whether Screen Time restrictions are preventing access.

Translation Availability

Some language pairs may require Apple translation support or a language package downloaded to your device. Follow the in-app download prompt or try another supported language pair.

Camera and Image Translation

Make sure Camera access is enabled before using camera translation. Access to an image is used only when you select an image through the system photo picker.

Translation Results

CrossLingo does not currently save translation results to a translation history or favorites list. Translation results should be copied or otherwise saved by you before leaving the relevant translation screen if you need to keep them.

Live Translator Pro

Live Translator Pro provides paid access to text translation, voice translation, camera translation, and image translation while the subscription remains active.

Weekly Access

Weekly Access costs $0.29 for the first week. After the first week, the subscription automatically renews at $9.99 per week until canceled.

Yearly Access

Yearly Access costs $59.99 per year and automatically renews annually until canceled.

The exact price, currency, billing period, applicable taxes, and renewal terms displayed in the App and Apple's purchase confirmation sheet apply to your purchase.

Restoring a Subscription

If you previously subscribed using the same Apple ID, tap Restore on the subscription screen. Restoring checks for an existing subscription and does not create a new purchase.

Canceling a Subscription

You can manage or cancel Live Translator Pro through your Apple ID subscription settings. To avoid the next renewal charge, cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current subscription period.

Deleting CrossLingo does not cancel an active subscription.

Refunds

Subscription payments, billing, cancellation, and refund decisions are handled by Apple under the applicable App Store rules.

Feedback and Developer Replies

Submitting Feedback

Open the Settings page in CrossLingo and tap Feedback to submit a question, report a problem, or share a suggestion with the developer team.

You may provide feedback details and, where available, optional contact information and up to three common image files such as PNG or JPEG.

Viewing Feedback History

Open Settings, tap Feedback, and then tap History to view feedback you previously submitted and replies from the developer team.

Unread Reply Notification

When the developer team sends a reply that you have not yet viewed, a red-dot notification may appear on the Feedback entry and the History entry.

Open History and view the relevant reply to update its read status.

Feedback Content

Do not include sensitive personal information, private conversations, payment-card details, passwords, authentication information, or confidential content in feedback text or attached images.

Contact Support

The recommended support method is the in-app Feedback feature. It allows you to review your feedback history and developer replies inside CrossLingo.

If you cannot access the App, contact us by email:

Crosslingo2026@outlook.com

When reporting a technical problem, please include your device model, iOS version, CrossLingo version, selected languages, and the steps required to reproduce the problem.

Do not send private conversations, confidential translation content, passwords, or payment information by email.
