CrossLingo Support

Last Updated: August 5, 2026 · Version 1.3

CrossLingo is a translation app for text, voice, camera, and image translation on iPhone. This page provides support information for Version 1.3, CrossLingo Plus, and the in-app Feedback feature.

Common Help Topics

Voice translation

Make sure Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions are enabled for CrossLingo in the iOS Settings app.

Siri and Dictation message

If iOS reports that Siri and Dictation are disabled, enable Dictation and Siri in system settings and check Screen Time restrictions.

Translation availability

Some language pairs may require Apple translation support or language downloads on the device. Try another language pair or follow the in-app download prompt.

Camera translation

Make sure Camera access is enabled. Photo Library access is only needed when you choose an image from the system photo picker.

CrossLingo Plus

Subscriptions are purchased through Apple In-App Purchase. Use Restore in the subscription screen if you already subscribed with the same Apple ID.

Subscription price or offer

The exact plan, introductory offer, price, billing period, renewal terms, and cancellation terms are shown in the App and in Apple's purchase confirmation sheet before you subscribe.

Canceling a subscription

Manage or cancel CrossLingo Plus from your Apple ID subscription settings. Deleting the App does not cancel an active subscription.

Feedback history

Open the Profile tab, tap Feedback, and use History to view submitted feedback and developer replies. A red dot means there is an unread reply.

Feedback images

You may attach up to three common image files such as PNG or JPEG. Please avoid uploading sensitive personal information, private conversations, payment details, passwords, or confidential content.

Contact Support

The recommended way to contact support is through the in-app Feedback page. It can include your app version and device context, and it lets you view reply history inside the App.
