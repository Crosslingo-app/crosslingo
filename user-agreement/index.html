7. Automatic Renewal, Cancellation, and Restore

Subscription charges are billed through your Apple ID after you confirm the purchase using Apple's purchase confirmation sheet.

The weekly subscription renews at $9.99 per week after the $0.29 first-week period unless canceled. The yearly subscription renews at $59.99 per year unless canceled.

To avoid the next renewal charge, cancel your subscription at least 24 hours before the end of the current subscription period through your Apple ID subscription settings.

Deleting CrossLingo does not cancel an active subscription.

Restore checks Apple purchase information and our subscription service for an eligible active subscription. Restoring does not create a new purchase.

Apple handles payment processing, billing, cancellation, and refund decisions. We do not receive your full payment-card details.
